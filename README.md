Story Nook a mobile app for users to keep track of books they have read. The app will also 
keep track of their current reading progresses, remember books that were recommended to 
them, let them set reading goals and help the user to discover new books to read based on 
other users’ opinions rather than from social algorithms.  

The app will be like a personal reading journal. A book tracker that is flexible to the irregular 
reading habits most of use have. The app will have structured reviews to give better and more 
precise recommendations. The app can also be used with friends to share book and discuss 
books in private reading circles. The app will be designed with the user in mind its focus is to 
make reading more social.
